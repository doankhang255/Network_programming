"The application needs to allow players to play chess against others in an online environment. The required features include:

-User login and authentication.
-View a list of online players.
-Match random players (only among those who choose the random match option when logging in).
-Match players by skill level (based on their achieved rating, only among those who choose the skill-based match option when logging in).
-Create game rooms, invite players to join the room, and accept invitations.
-Take turns moving the chess pieces, with validation to ensure legal moves.
-Check and announce the winner or loser for both parties.
-Propose a draw, accept or decline draw proposals.
-Calculate player ratings, with a rating system determined by the server.
-View player rankings based on their ratings."
